# Transformation registry

Eight Character-form cards plus one Ability-established fusion state. Distinct game forms have explicit base IDs and entry conditions. The source references distinguish official pages from precise scene verification still pending.

## CS1-027 — Sora, Final Form

Alternate Battle Form. Replace the exact base CS1-001 in your Main Phase with empty Menu, paying printed resources. The base must have at least two differently named Equipment attached. If transforming in Center, reserve another registered exact base copy under D011.

Cost 3 Light / 2 Darkness;2 arsenal slots. 8500 ATK / 21000 HP before Center bonus.

## CS1-341 — Interlink

Fusion State established by Ability. Linked temporary battle state. Active gains ATK/HP; partner cannot act. Does not grant Transformed protection or reset damage/attack usage.



Some source-game transformations (Personas as manifestations, biological mutations as standalone identities) are deliberately represented through other card types or base states. A costume alone receives no Transformation rules.
