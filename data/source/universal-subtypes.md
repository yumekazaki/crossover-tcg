Nature/species tags with a signature franchise (Nobody, Heartless, Blade species, B.O.W., etc.) remain queryable subtypes and are indexed in species-nature-registry.md. Affiliations are separate and never silently substitute for a subtype. The JSON registry is the canonical machine index.
