BUILD STATUS

What works in this build

Imported source data is not the same as implemented or tested game functionality. This view lists the current boundaries.

Imported

The original 500-card set and the separate 50-card Harry Potter (Anime) fan set are available with the recovered master rules, decision history, and subtype/form registries.

Implemented slice

Deck checks, setup/mulligans, standard Party deployment, paid compatible Equipment attachment, response Menus, two healing effects, turn resources, basic chain combat and defeat/life handling.

Not complete

Most effects and all Finishers remain blocked. Eight transformation entry paths work, but their printed form abilities and voluntary reversion remain pending; trigger/delay/protection systems, full effect negation, persistence, accounts, AI, and online matches are not implemented.

Playtest limits

New Harry Potter (Anime) card effects are visible in the library and can be added to deck lists, but their effects are not implemented in the match engine. Matches use one shared browser, decks stay in that browser, and no accounts, AI opponent, or remote multiplayer backend is connected.
