# Crossover TCG application dashboard

## Isolation and sources

- New independent local Git repository: initialized.
- Vanguard project: untouched; never used as the workspace or baseline.
- Rules, decision history, registries and complete card database: imported from the current crossover design workspace.
- GitHub repository: public `yumekazaki/crossover-tcg`; deployed through GitHub Pages at https://yumekazaki.github.io/crossover-tcg/.
- Card artwork integration: library cards, card detail panels and local-match dialogs render an approved local image when available. Approval, local-file existence and one-image-per-card uniqueness are required.
- Card images renderable in this repository: 0 of 500. Records label 163 images as attached, but their local files are absent and none establishes redistribution approval. Character art must depict the specific character/form, including Roxas, Xion, Axel and other Organization members individually; no repeated group image.

Engine activated-effect coverage is **13 of 500** (`CS1-281`, `CS1-282`, `CS1-285`, `CS1-290`, `CS1-311`, `CS1-316`, `CS1-319`, `CS1-320`, `CS1-334`, `CS1-339`, `CS1-394`, `CS1-433`, `CS1-436`); a separate explicit trigger program covers Mickey (`CS1-011`). These seventeen cards are marked mechanically validated in the card data based on focused automated tests; none is balance-reviewed or playtested. Firaga uses a shared damage/defeat transaction and its effect damage does not cause combat life loss or Momentum. Stocked Memories reveals up to four actual top-deck instances, supports an optional Ability add, a hand-to-bottom choice and player-ordered bottoming of the remaining reveals, then resumes the Menu. Union Cross requires two differently named Keyblade Wielders and a hand discard, then reveals up to five cards and can Add an untransformed Sword Wielder to an empty Party Zone despite the normal Add being used; it suppresses that Character's Specials until its controller's next turn and bottoms the remaining reveals in chosen order. Dorm Study Session conditionally requires a Character discard when a Persona User is controlled, reveals five and optionally Adds an untransformed Support or Healer to an empty Party Zone; that Character cannot attack and has its Specials suppressed until its controller's next turn, then the remaining revealed cards are bottomed in chosen order. Setup now requires a chooser action followed by first-player-ordered, physical-copy starting Main selections; only then are Main Decks shuffled, opening hands dealt and mulligans enabled. The other card effects and triggers remain unsupported. The engine records last-known battlefield/Equipment snapshots for damage defeats, queues eligible trigger groups FIFO, opens response Menus in turn-player placement order, and pauses resolving Menus for choices without granting priority. Mickey's trigger heals the current Main and, with no Party Members, draws then requires a player-selected discard. Party Swap passes leave/return event snapshots through the collector, though no current program matches those event types. Trigger coverage remains partial: event families, optional-trigger decisions, same-controller ordering choices, suspension, and pre-damage combat triggers are not complete. Three Character Specials are mechanically validated: Axel (`CS1-005`) discards a hand card to deal 2000 effect damage to up to two opposing Party Members simultaneously; Ashley Graham (`CS1-177`) returns a damaged Party Member and its Equipment without refund, then may replace it from hand with a matching universal subtype, granting Momentum when that replacement shares a franchise with another ally. Grace Ashcroft (`CS1-178`) reveals five, may add an Ability, bottoms the remainder in chosen order, conditionally gains Light or Darkness based on the selected Ability requirement, then banks a one-Momentum discount for an Ability requiring a subtype on a different-franchise ally. The discount lasts until end of turn and is consumed by the next matching Ability activation. Aether Channel and Theurgy Charge add team-checked Light, Darkness and Momentum lines; Blade Awakening converts a Driver plus a hand cost into a suppressed Blade Party Member without consuming standard Add. Eight canonical forms can enter play using explicit base, resource, entry-condition, reserve, Equipment-transfer and defeat/restore rules, but their printed form abilities and voluntary reversion are still unimplemented. Voluntary unequip, Finisher execution, full protection/suspension/control resolution, AI, accounts, online matches and deployment remain incomplete. Center switching and Party Swap resolve through response Menus; automated checks cover HP adjustment, per-turn use, Momentum payment, fresh hand instances, transformed-target rejection, and the unique printed-cost refund. The test suite currently has 54 checks (17 data, 37 engine). `docs/effect-coverage-plan.md` groups the remaining card text into proposed implementation families without marking those effects implemented. This dashboard distinguishes imported, implemented, validated, reviewed and actually played work.
