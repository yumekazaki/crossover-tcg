# Set 02: Harry Potter (Anime)

This is a fan-made, anime-style alternate-universe set. It is not an official anime adaptation. The user's requested Draconia Malfoy interpretation replaces Draco Malfoy throughout Set 02; no card in this set names Draco Malfoy.

## Set contents

- 50 unique cards: 22 Characters, 10 Equipment, 8 Abilities, 6 Speed Abilities and 4 Finishers.
- Harry Potter appears on three individual Character cards. Draconia Malfoy appears on three individual Character cards. Two additional Character cards feature them together.
- Hermione Granger, Ron Weasley, Voldemort, Dumbledore, Snape, Luna, Neville, Ginny, Cedric and Hagrid each have representation.
- Eight distinct AI-generated artworks are attached to the Harry, Draconia and duo Character cards. No asset path is shared. Other Set 02 cards show named pending-art placeholders.
- A validated 40-card sample list, **Scarlet & Emerald Counterspell**, is registered for local practice.

## Play pattern

The set is built around sequencing effects through response Menus. Harry rewards prepared Speed Abilities, protects allies and preserves a card for a timed counter. Draconia rewards deployment and deck selection, redirects targeted effects and repositions cards for Momentum. Their pair cards and shared Equipment turn those actions into attack-chain discounts, resource bursts and conditional counterspells. Hermione and Ron provide deck selection, hand filtering and guard support; Voldemort and Horcrux cards form a separate Darkness/recursion plan.

Universal subtypes such as Mage, Tactician, Guardian, Support and Wand Wielder let these cards mix with the original crossover pool. Slytherin, Gryffindor, Seeker and Horcrux tags offer narrower internal synergies.
