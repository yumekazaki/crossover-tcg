# Engine audit: highest-priority next work

Audit basis: current `src/engine/game.js`, `tests/engine.test.js`, `data/source/master-rulebook.md`, and `docs/rules-engine-contract.md`. This is a focused review of implemented paths, not a certification of the full rules engine.

**Damage update:** Combat and the first targeted effect-damage card now share a transaction that applies damage before defeat handling, preserves Main revival/Equipment return and records last-known snapshots. Firaga's effect damage does not apply combat life loss or Momentum. Multi-target effect damage, prevention/redirection and general trigger ordering remain incomplete.

**Transformation update:** All eight canonical transformation IDs now have explicit entry-condition programs. The engine supports paid Main Phase activation, exact-base checks, Main reserve search/shuffle, legal Equipment transfer, full-HP form entry, and transformed Main and Party defeat handling. Printed form abilities, voluntary reversion, and trigger interactions remain unverified or unsupported.

**Phase-window update:** Empty phase windows require two consecutive passes. Combat defeat collection and the narrow Mickey trigger path are implemented; the queue blocks advancement while pending triggers remain. Broad trigger families, trigger choice/order and combat pre-damage checkpoints remain open.

**Movement update:** `SWITCH_CENTER` now opens a standard response Menu, consumes the once-per-own-turn allowance on activation, rejects transformed Characters, exchanges the Center/Party positions, and applies the ±13,000 current/max HP adjustments. `PARTY_SWAP` requires 6 Momentum up front, selects one or two untransformed Party instances, returns legal portions through fresh hand instances, and refunds each returned Equipment's printed Light/Darkness components using the D004 rounding rule. Both actions are exposed to the local practice UI and covered by automated tests. Trigger collection for Party Swap remains unavailable because the event scheduler is not implemented; this limitation is tracked rather than silently treated as complete.

**Combo resource update:** `CS1-316` Aether Channel and `CS1-319` Theurgy Charge now have explicit Main Phase programs. They validate the required differently named subtype team, resolve gains through their Menu, and apply the printed attachment/Finisher lock until the next turn. Theurgy's chosen card is moved to the Main Deck bottom as an activation cost before the Menu opens. General Finisher execution remains unsupported, so its turn lock is stored but cannot yet gate an executable Finisher action.

**Equipment resource update:** `CS1-311` Armiger Preparation is implemented for a Sword Wielder with at least three attached Equipment. It grants one Light and one Darkness, then suppresses abilities on the next Equipment successfully attached that turn until its controller's next turn begins. The duration, resource gain and attachment selection are covered by a focused test. Equipment effect activation remains otherwise unsupported.

**Deck selection update:** `CS1-285` Stocked Memories and `CS1-290` Union Cross now use the staged top-deck choice path. Union Cross validates two differently named Keyblade Wielders, pays a hand discard before activation, then offers an untransformed Sword Wielder for an empty Party Zone; it suppresses that Character's Specials through the controller's next turn and bottoms the rest in player-chosen order. Automated tests cover requirement rejection without paying costs, successful deployment after standard Add, zone ordering and suppression expiry. This remains narrow coverage: search-to-Party families need their own exact requirements and effects.

**Persona deck-selection update:** `CS1-320` Dorm Study Session now shares the staged reveal choice flow while retaining its distinct rules: a controlled Persona User makes a Character discard mandatory; otherwise it has no discard cost. It offers only untransformed Support or Healer Characters, suppresses their Specials and attacks until the controller's next turn begins, and bottoms the remaining reveals in chosen order. Tests cover both conditional-cost branches, eligibility, and the attack lock.

**Hand-to-Party combo update:** `CS1-339` Blade Awakening now reveals a hand Blade, pays an Ability discard cost to Continue?, then Adds the Blade to an empty Party Zone at full HP through its resolving Menu Select. It bypasses the standard Add counter and stores Character Special suppression until that player's next turn begins. Deck-search/reveal choices and trigger-driven placement remain separate unsupported families.

**Cross-franchise combo update:** `CS1-334` Heart-to-Heart requires two distinct allied Characters from different franchises. It restores 2500 HP to each if both are injured; otherwise it restores 3500 HP to the injured Character, if any, and gains one Momentum. The engine offers only legal pairs and rechecks them at resolution. Focused tests cover the one-injured and both-injured branches; no playtest or balance review is claimed.

**Character Special update:** Axel (`CS1-005`) is the first executable active Character Special. During its controller's Main Phase, it discards one hand card up front, then opens a response Menu with up to two distinct opposing Party Characters chosen as targets. At resolution, still-legal targets take 2000 simultaneous effect damage through the shared damage transaction. The Character stays on the battlefield; its name usage is limited for the turn. This is one Special program, not general Special support.

**Movement Special update:** Ashley Graham (`CS1-177`) now targets a different untransformed Party Member missing at least 4000 HP, returns it to hand as a fresh battlefield identity, and returns attached Equipment to its owners' arsenals without refund. A paused Menu choice may reveal and Add a hand Character that shares one of the returned card's universal combat subtypes; the replacement enters at full HP with its Specials suppressed until its controller's next turn begins. If that replacement shares a franchise with another ally, its controller gains one Momentum. Automated tests cover subtype filtering, the no-refund Equipment move, fresh physical-copy identity, suppression and the franchise Momentum branch. Other character movement and Special programs remain unsupported.

**Trigger scheduler update:** `CS1-011` Mickey is registered for allied Character defeats. Combat captures last-known Character/Equipment records before zone changes; eligible triggers enter FIFO groups and resolve through their own response Menus after the current Menu finishes. Mickey's no-Party branch pauses resolution for the controller to choose a discard, without priority or responses. Party Swap records leave/return event timing. This is a narrow trigger implementation: simultaneous ordering choices among multiple triggers, optional collection, suspension, broad event matching, and pre-damage combat trigger checkpoints remain open.

## P0 — Current combat and trigger status

1. **Damage and Main defeat revival — implemented for Battle combat and targeted Firaga.** Combat commits assigned damage together; Firaga uses the shared transaction for its single target. Defeat handling snapshots affected Characters, returns attached Equipment, creates a fresh Main battlefield instance at full HP, and checks victory. Combat applies printed life/Momentum consequences; effect-origin damage does not. Multi-target effect damage and broad damage modifiers remain unsupported. Authority: `master-rulebook.md:58-60,137`; rules contract “Combat transaction”, “HP, movement, and Equipment”, and “Win conditions”.

2. **Combat guard and response priority — implemented for the current combat path.** The defender receives one pre-response guard declaration checkpoint; after confirmation, the response Menu begins with the attacker and alternates. Tests verify that guard declaration closes before responses and that attacker priority follows. Additional speed/Finisher windows remain incomplete. Authority: `master-rulebook.md:89,127,131-133`.

3. **Combat's simultaneous damage core is implemented; general event and trigger ordering is not.** Damage recipients' HP is committed together; all resulting defeats are identified before life/Momentum consequences and terminal-state evaluation. The narrow after-combat Mickey trigger path collects and resolves after a combat defeat. General trigger records, pre-damage combat checkpoints, event-specific trigger ordering and full D008 scheduling remain incomplete. Authority: `master-rulebook.md:55-60,123,133,137`; rules contract “Combat transaction” and “Win conditions”.

## P1 — Implement the next engine foundations

4. **Turn and phase checkpoints — empty-window passes and one trigger path are implemented.** `PASS_PRIORITY` now alternates priority and advances a phase only after two consecutive passes. Eligible Mickey defeat triggers are queued, opened, and resolved FIFO after the active Menu; a paused mandatory choice prevents any intervening action. Other trigger records, beginning/end resource/draw triggers, End Phase expiry, optional collection, ordering choices, and suspension remain open. Authority: `master-rulebook.md:22-27,99-110,123`; rules contract “Priority and timing”.

5. **Setup order — chooser and starting Main selection implemented.** Every new match begins with `CHOOSE_FIRST_PLAYER`, offered only to the randomly selected chooser. Players then select distinct physical nontransformed Character copies in first-player order. Only after both choices does the engine shuffle each remaining Main Deck, deal five cards and open mulligans. Starting Main options are built from current registered physical copies, and an invalid selection cannot advance setup. Authority: `master-rulebook.md:119` (D001).

6. **Equipment passive effects — regex execution removed; no Equipment programs implemented.** Combat now consults an explicit `getEquipmentProgram()` registry rather than parsing card text. No Equipment program is registered, so all current Equipment effects remain disabled. Modifier duration, source/controller, global reductions, prevention, redirection, and compatible ordering still need a layered combat model. Authority: rules contract “Combat transaction”, “HP, movement, and Equipment”, and unresolved U004/U008.

## P2 — Expand only after the above

7. **Voluntary unequip, effect negation, protection/suspension, delayed effects, and Finishers have no executable commands.** Center switch and Party Swap are now implemented. Party Swap leave/return event snapshots reach the trigger collector, but no current program matches those event types. Transformation entry, Main base restoration, and Party form defeat are covered; voluntary reversion remains unsupported. Build protection/suspension only over an explicit duration/trigger queue and retain U003-U010 as unresolved where the rulebook says so.

8. **Acceptance tests cover a growing set of narrow engine cases.** They now verify setup, mulligan, Add, Axel and Ashley Character Specials, hand discard, simultaneous damage, optional matching-subtype Party replacement, healing and response interruption, staged Stocked Memories deck choice, five resource/deployment/cross-franchise combo programs, Center switch/Party Swap, response and guard windows, chain costs, Equipment cost, combat/effect-damage Main revival, simultaneous combat defeats, transformation requirements, Main restoration, Party form defeat and blocked unregistered Equipment effects. Most other rulebook invariants remain untested. Add tests per rule capability as each foundation lands. Test counts are not evidence that the whole master rulebook is implemented.

## Recommended next implementation slice

The full D001 setup choice sequence is now covered. The first shared damage transaction slice is Firaga; next expand to a tested multi-target effect-damage cohort and explicit prevention/redirection handling while keeping combat-only life/Momentum consequences separated by origin.
